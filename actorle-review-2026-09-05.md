two independent directions.

## What actually made it work (and what won't repeat)

1. **Timing.** Launched in the 2022 Wordle wave. 46 "-dle" games launched in 2022; the
   directories now list **1,000+**, and direct clones die within a year. The 2025-26
   breakouts (Clues by Sam at 50k daily players, putt.day) have *different mechanics*, not a
   new theme on the same mechanic.
2. **The share text.** Every result is a copyable emoji grid that fits a tweet. That, not
   SEO, was the growth engine. Laszlo says so directly.
3. **A free, licensed, evergreen dataset.** TMDB means the daily puzzle costs nothing to
   author. One person, no content treadmill.
4. **Near-zero cost, so it never had to die.** $50/month means a declining game still pays
   for itself for years and can be revived with a new mode.
5. **Portfolio, not product.** Once one game has an audience, the second launches to it.
   Cross-promo links carry referral tags so he knows what sends traffic.

## Honest economics for a new entrant

The traffic estimate and the revenue figure together give a realized rate:

| | |
|---|---|
| Actorle's realized ad rate | **~$3.50-4 per 1,000 pageviews** ($3k ÷ ~880k pageviews/month) |
| Pageviews needed for $3k/month at that rate | **~750k/month**, roughly **8-10k daily visitors** at 3 pages each |
| Ad networks by threshold | Journey by Mediavine: 1,000 sessions/mo (70% share). Mediavine: 50k sessions. Playwire: 500k+ pageviews. AdSense: no minimum, lowest rate |
| Median outcome of a new -dle | **$0-100/month** |
| Upside if it catches | **$1-5k/month** for a few evenings of build and ~$0/month to run |

Rates for a tech audience will be lower still: 40-60% of PC gamers run an ad blocker
(general population ~33%). Affiliate links are immune to ad blockers, which is why the
recommended idea has one.

So this is a cheap lottery ticket with a real but small chance of a meaningful payout. That
is a fine thing to hold *alongside* Longview. It is not a plan B for the $20k target.

## What to take to the Longview site (small, does not change the plan)

1. **A shareable result object.** The home page already has sliders producing an
   illustrative estimate. Turn that into the plan's own opener: a **Lost Revenue
   Calculator** (leads/month × average job × current response time → dollars per year) with a
   copyable one-line result and a permalink that carries the three inputs, so the number
   travels in a text or a Facebook group post. It makes the cold-email sentence self-serve
   and gives every cold email a link worth clicking.
2. **Tag every link.** Actorle tags cross-links so it knows what works. Every cold email,
   directory listing, and social post should carry a UTM so the first 50 emails teach us
   which opener converts.
3. **Publish the measurement.** The distribution doc already plans a "what response times
   look like in [metro]" page from our own data. That is the Actorle move: a page people in
   the trade share among themselves. Build it after the first 20 measurements, not before.

Not applicable: streaks, archives, daily return hooks. Longview is a one-shot decision, not
a habit. Don't force it.
